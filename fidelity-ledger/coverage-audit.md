## Source quality and synthesis limits

The supplied Markdown has OCR artifacts, occasional missing ligatures and, especially in Recruitment, broken two-column/table ordering. The original Recruitment PDF yielded readable text and a rendered page was inspected to confirm column order; key chapters were then read by PDF file page. Scaling People PDF pages additionally corroborated organizational concepts. Dalton's PDF lacks text on a sampled page; the usable supplied Markdown carries its workflow, so no claim of full PDF OCR is made. Other noisy wording was checked by bounded context; unreadable details were not used as quantitative evidence.

No current job-market, salary, visa, employer-policy or research-ranking facts were imported from books. No browsing-based current factual claims were required for authoring this methodological layer. Functional role examples and the integrated career assessment are original synthesis. Historical company examples are bounded. All new sources are English; the existing explicit English default is preserved.
