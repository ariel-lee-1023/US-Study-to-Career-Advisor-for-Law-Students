## Observed responses

Two separate evaluators received the same case records and questions, one with the prior core and one with the revised core. Each could use the same twelve modules. The evaluators saw neither a model answer nor the other response.

- **LL.M. deposit.** Both chose South. Its director confirmed responsibility for a privacy incident workflow and it cost $18,000 less. Both traced the competing school's 88% figure to one pooled release, recognized the alumna's prior employer as her route into work and identified evidence that could justify paying the premium. The revised response also explicitly distinguished confidence in the practicum choice from confidence in eventual hiring.
- **Changed evidence.** In a follow-up, North supplied a signed individual assignment with supervised case reviews and a guaranteed $24,000 net stipend. Both switched to North, computed its $6,000 advantage and left their interpretation of the pooled employment figure unchanged.
- **Recruiter reply.** Both recommended applying before the closing date. The revised response explained why a team reply was hardly expected before application and identified a more informative future signal.
- **Sentence-only edit.** Both delivered a single sentence. The revised response converted a short chat into a 15-minute request. The final core therefore explicitly preserves the writer's level of specificity and commitments in a narrow rewrite.

A fresh evaluator used that final wording on two further requests. It retained the open-ended duration of a casual chat. In a decision between an introduction available only that day and repairing a memo, it favored taking the introduction first. It used three documented pre-read screenings as evidence about access, credited the separate manager's specific criticism as evidence about the memo, and explained why time to revise before the assessment allowed both goals to be pursued.

These observations come from one response per case and version tested. Both versions reached sound decisions in the paired cases. The earlier seventeen-case acceptance suite and any estimate of general performance gains remain outside this exercise.
